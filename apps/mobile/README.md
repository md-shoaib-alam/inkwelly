# Welcome to your Expo app 👋

This is an [Expo](https://expo.dev) project created with [`create-expo-app`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/create-expo-app).

## Get started

1. Install dependencies

   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

2. Start the app

   ```bash
   npx expo start
   ```

In the output, you'll find options to open the app in a

- [development build](https://docs.expo.dev/develop/development-builds/introduction/)
- [Android emulator](https://docs.expo.dev/workflow/android-studio-emulator/)
- [iOS simulator](https://docs.expo.dev/workflow/ios-simulator/)
- [Expo Go](https://expo.dev/go), a limited sandbox for trying out app development with Expo

You can start developing by editing the files inside the **app** directory. This project uses [file-based routing](https://docs.expo.dev/router/introduction).

## Get a fresh project

When you're ready, run:

```bash
npm run reset-project
```

This command will move the starter code to the **app-example** directory and create a blank **app** directory where you can start developing.

### Other setup steps

- To set up ESLint for linting, run `npx expo lint`, or follow our guide on ["Using ESLint and Prettier"](https://docs.expo.dev/guides/using-eslint/)

## Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **test-app** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App") built for school management.

### 1. Information We Collect
We collect only the minimum amount of information necessary to provide the school management services:
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, role (Superadmin, Admin, Staff, Teacher, Student, Parent), and school-related identifiers.
- **Academic and Communication Data:** Class information, assignments, grades, announcements, and messages sent within the App.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** We collect device tokens via Expo Notifications to deliver school-related announcements and alerts.
- **Device & Usage Data:** Basic technical logs collected by Firebase/Google Services for stability and performance monitoring.

### 2. Purpose of Collection (Data Minimization & Purpose Limitation)
We use the collected data strictly for:
- Authentication and authorization of school management roles.
- Facilitating communication between parents, students, teachers, and admins.
- Sending important updates and notification alerts.
- Monitoring and improving app stability.
*We do not use your personal data for advertising or marketing, nor do we sell it to third parties.*

### 3. Data Storage and Security
- **Data Security:** All communications between the App and the server are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
- **Device Storage:** We use secure local storage (`@react-native-async-storage/async-storage`) to keep session tokens and user preference data locally.
- **Retention Period:** We retain your account data for as long as your school account is active or as required by your educational institution. 

### 4. Third-Party Services
Our App integrates the following third-party services:
- **Expo & Expo Notifications:** To compile, run, and deliver push notifications.
- **Google Firebase:** For backend services, analytics, and notification management.

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)
Since our App is used by educational institutions, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and family educational privacy regulations:
- For students under 13, account registration is managed and authorized directly by the school administration or with verified parental consent.
- We do not collect or request more personal information from children than is reasonably necessary to participate in the school activities.

### 6. Your Rights (Access, Correction, and Deletion)
In compliance with global standards (such as GDPR, CCPA, and LGPD), users (or their parents/guardians) have the right to:
- **Access & Portability:** Request a copy of all personal data held.
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or outdated information.
- **Erasure (Account Deletion):** Request the deletion of their account and all associated personal data. To request deletion, please contact your school administrator or email us directly.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and posting the new policy in the App metadata or README.

### 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions or requests regarding your privacy, please contact us.
